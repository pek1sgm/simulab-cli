### 1 Task
The objective of this analysis was to verify the system stability for the Jaguar Land Rover (JLR) MLA-H MY28 project. A VSSP Tuning Check (TC) was performed using software version 450_700_C_08_00 with hardware library H2E_AEI_A_10_01_C70. Vehicle data for the initial work packages (SWP/1/ and SWP/2/) was recorded using a Range Rover L460 (LHD, iVAR).

> [1] no report – [2] VT AUTOSAR tuning cannot be applied to vECU – [3] all stable, some VM<0.4 – [4] stability VM<0.4 not explicitly tested

* **SWP/1/ & SWP/2/ (Vehicle Testing):** Marginal tune and plant identification were successfully completed using an extended test catalogue that included circle driving and hands-on steering conditions.
* **SWP/3/ (CAE Plant Identification):** The marginal tunes identified in vehicle testing result in minimal Vector Margin (VM) offsets, confirming validity of the plant model and allowing to proceed subsequent VSSP steps.
* **SWP/4/ (CAE Controls Validation):** A minor deviation between simulation and vehicle test data was observed in the Nyquist stability analysis. This discrepancy was attributed to a mismatch in AUTOSAR tuning parameters between the virtual ECU (vECU) and the vehicle's marginal tune. As the final software tuning was not modified for subsequent steps, this deviation was not considered a blocker.
* **SWP/5/ (CAE Stability DOE):** The stability DOE confirmed that the system is stable across all tested operating conditions with the 450_700_C_08_00 tuning. However, certain test points violated the internal Bosch requirement of VM > 0.4. These conditions were identified as highly specific and non-representative of real-world driving:
  * **Asphalt:** Hands-off, on-center, slow driving (<10 km/h) with a torsion bar torque (TBT) > 1.2 Nm.
  * **Hoist:** Hands-off, on-center, slow driving (10-30 km/h) with a TBT > 1.2 Nm.
* **SWP/6/ (Vehicle Validation):** The final on-vehicle validation for the most critical conditions identified in simulation was not performed.

---

### 3 Conclusion
The VSSP tuning analysis confirms that software version 450_700_C_08_00 is stable for all operating conditions investigated. While the system violates the internal Bosch stability requirement (VM > 0.4) under a few specific, high-torque, low-speed scenarios, these cases are considered theoretical and do not represent a risk of sustained oscillation in real-world driving. Based on this engineering assessment, the current tuning is judged robust and acceptable for the application.

##### 4.2.5 SWP/5/: Stability DOE

* **Asphalt most Critical Condition:** Hands off – Center - Slow driving <10 km/h - TBT > 1.2Nm

**Summary:**
* Hands off more critical than hands on
* On center more critical than off center
* Hoist more critical than asphalt
* Lowest VM values are in the frequency range 17-20Hz
